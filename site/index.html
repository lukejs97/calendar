Minuteman

Your calendar, ready at a minute's notice.

Minuteman is a personal calendar app, in the style of Fantastical, for the Mac, Windows and the web. It shows the events in your Google calendars, from one or several Google accounts, together with your Todoist tasks, and on a Mac your Apple Calendar events and Reminders.

What it does

  • Day, multi-day, week, month, year and agenda views.
  • Add events and tasks by typing a sentence, such as “Lunch with Sam tomorrow 1pm at Cafe Roma”.
  • Edit, move and delete events; see their details, guests and video-call links.
  • Search, reminders, working offline, and a small calendar in the menu bar.
  • Contacts, in the style of Cardhop: find, add and edit your Google contacts by typing (“Jane Doe jane@example.com 555-0100”), or say “call Sam”, “email Sam” or “meet Sam tomorrow 3pm”.

Your Google data

Minuteman asks Google for access to your calendars and contacts so it can show your events and contacts and add, change or delete the ones you ask it to. Your data is used only for that, isn't sold or shared, and isn't used for ads. Details are in the privacy policy.

Source code

Minuteman is a personal project. Its code is public on GitHub, where you can also ask questions or report problems.
