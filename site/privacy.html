Privacy policy

Minuteman

Last updated October 29, 2026.

Minuteman is a personal calendar app. This page explains what information it uses, where that information is kept, and who it's shared with. In short: your information is used only to show and manage your own calendar and tasks. It isn't sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Information Minuteman uses

  • Google Calendar. With your permission, Minuteman reads your calendars and events (titles, times, places, descriptions, guests and video-call links) so it can show them, and adds, changes or deletes events when you ask it to. It also learns your Google account's email address, to tell your accounts apart.
  • Todoist, if you connect it: your tasks and projects, to show them and to add, change, complete or delete tasks when you ask.
  • Apple Calendar and Reminders (Mac only, if you turn them on): your events and reminders, read on your Mac.
  • Weather, if you turn it on: the town you choose (its name while you search for it, then its coordinates), or, if you choose your current location, your approximate location (to about a kilometre), is sent to Open-Meteo to get the forecast. Nothing else about you is sent.
  • Your location, if you use it for the weather (above) or, on a Mac, turn on “Time to leave”: for that, your Mac's approximate location and an event's place are sent to Apple Maps to get the driving time.

Where it's kept

  • Desktop app (Mac and Windows): your sign-ins are kept in your computer's keychain (macOS Keychain or Windows Credential Manager), and a copy of recent events and tasks is saved on your computer so Minuteman works offline. Nothing is sent to any server of Minuteman's; the app talks directly to Google, Todoist and, on a Mac, Apple.
  • Web version: it runs on Cloudflare. Your Google and Todoist sign-ins are stored there, encrypted, so the site can reach your calendar for you; your browser only gets a sign-in cookie. Events and tasks are fetched when you look at them and aren't stored on the server.

Sharing

Minuteman doesn't sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone. Your information goes only to the services it comes from (Google, Todoist, Apple), to Open-Meteo for the weather if you turn it on, and, for the web version, to Cloudflare, which hosts it. There are no analytics or trackers.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Minuteman's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your information

  • Remove a Google account in Minuteman under Settings → Google accounts, and disconnect Todoist in Settings. This deletes their sign-ins.
  • You can also revoke Minuteman's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • The desktop app's saved copy of your events is in its cache folder: on a Mac, ~/Library/Caches/com.lukesassa.calendar; on Windows, %LOCALAPPDATA%\com.lukesassa.calendar. Delete that folder to remove it.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. For questions, open an issue on GitHub.
